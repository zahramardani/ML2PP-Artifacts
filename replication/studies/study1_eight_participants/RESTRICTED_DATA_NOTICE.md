# Restricted study materials

The following materials are intentionally not published in this public
repository:

- completed participant forms and raw scans;
- names, email addresses, signatures, and consent records;
- original document and image metadata;
- participant-level free-text responses;
- nested archives and duplicate working files;
- any mapping between pseudonyms and real identities.

These exclusions protect participant confidentiality and avoid presenting
raw records as open data without a documented release basis. The public CSV
files contain only the de-identified values reported in the thesis.

Researchers seeking additional evidence should contact the corresponding
author. Access, if possible, remains subject to the original consent,
institutional requirements, and applicable data-protection rules.
