## Validation outcome

- River: 13,715 observed midnight records before 7 August 2022. A complete daily index contains 13,734 timestamps, so 19 calendar dates are absent. Six non-daily test rows dated 9 July 2025 and three empty columns found in the supplied file were excluded.
- Smart home: the previously deposited CSV was removed on 13 August 2026 after it was found to contain minute-averaged `humidity` values labelled as `use_kW`. The corrected reproducible transformation selects the actual source variable `use [kW]`, averages each consecutive 60 one-second observations, and produces 4,200 one-minute values from 1 January 2016 05:00 through 4 January 2016 02:59 UTC. The corrected CSV was independently regenerated and validated; its SHA-256 is recorded above and in the use-case directory.
- Solar: 796 observed hourly records from 15 May through 17 June 2020. A complete hourly index has 816 timestamps, revealing 20 absent hours. Night-time zero production is preserved.

These are processed research subsets. Their data-quality, transformation, and provenance boundaries are documented in the corresponding subdirectory README and PDF data card.
